Word lists: SCOWL

The game's bonus words and the daily puzzle's words come from SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists) by Kevin Atkinson, sizes 50 and below. Most of those words come from public-domain sources (the Moby Words II lexicon, Brian Kelk's UK English Wordlist and Alan Beale's 12Dicts), with American spellings from VarCon and word forms from an inflection database made with WordNet.
