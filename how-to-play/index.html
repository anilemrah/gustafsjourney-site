Gustaf's Journey · How to play

How to play: crosswords, hints and handy tips

Every level of Gustaf's Journey is a small crossword. Here's how it works, what the buttons do, and a few tips for when a word just won't come.

Free · iPhone & iPad

The basics

  1. Look at the letter wheelAt the bottom of the screen sit the letters for this level. Every word in the crossword can be spelled from them.
  2. Make a wordSlide your finger from letter to letter and lift it when the word is done. Or tap the letters one by one, then tap ✓. Words need three letters or more, and ✕ clears the word if you change your mind.
  3. Fill the crosswordA word that belongs in the puzzle drops into its place in the grid. Find every word and the level is complete.
  4. Enjoy the memoryEach finished level brings back a memory from Gustaf's life, with a Swedish word and its English meaning.

Bonus words

Found a real word that isn't in the crossword? It still counts: it's a bonus word, and every bonus word earns you a coin. They add up faster than you'd think.

Stuck? Shuffle or take a hint

  • Shuffle mixes up the letters on the wheel. Seeing them in a new order often shows you a word you missed, and it's free.
  • Hint fills in one letter of the crossword for 15 coins. With Gustaf's Journey Pro, an optional subscription, you get 3 free hints every day.

Coins

You start with 30 coins. You earn 10 for every level, 25 at each checkpoint on the map, 20 for the daily puzzle and 1 for every bonus word. After some levels you can choose to watch a short video to double the coins from that level. It's always up to you.

The map, the trunk and the cookbook

Your levels sit on an illustrated map of the places in Gustaf's life, from Lake Siljan to Minnesota. Checkpoints along the road hold keepsakes for Gustaf's trunk: photographs, letters and outfits for Kantis. Side trips open as you go, and every dish that turns up in the story is added to Gustaf's cookbook.

Journeys of the month, such as Lucia, Christmas, the Vasaloppet and Midsummer, open on their dates once you've finished your first 5 levels.

The daily puzzle

There's a fresh puzzle every day, with a Swedish word of the day and 20 coins. Your streak is counted kindly: a single missed day is forgiven.

Tips for solving

  1. Find the longest word first. Every level has at least one word that uses all the letters on the wheel. Find it and a big piece of the crossword falls into place.
  2. Then pick off the short ones. Three-letter words are quick to find and fill in letters for the longer words.
  3. Use the grid. A word's length, and any letters already in place, narrow down what it can be.
  4. Try endings. In English, plurals with S and endings like ED, ER and ING turn up all the time.
  5. Shuffle before you spend coins. A new order is often all it takes.
  6. Take a break. There's no clock. Put the phone down and come back later: the game remembers the words you've already found.

A little companyKantis cheers when you find a word, chats about Gustaf's memories, and gets a new outfit for every season. Tap Kantis on the home screen to say hello.
