Privacy Policy

Gustaf's Journey

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This privacy policy applies to Gustaf's Journey, a story word game for iPhone and iPad (“the App”, “we”, “us”). We collect only what the App needs to work and to get better, and we never sell your personal information.

Playing without an account

You can play the whole game without signing in. Your progress (levels, coins, keepsakes, Swedish words, a level you left half-way, and your settings) is then stored only on your device.

What we collect

  • Account information (optional). If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google to save your progress, we store the account identifier your sign-in method provides, and the name or email address it shares with us. Sign-in is handled by Supabase.
  • Saved progress (optional). When you are signed in, your progress is saved to your account so it can be restored on another iPhone or iPad.
  • Game usage and error reports. To understand which levels are too hard and to fix problems, the App sends simple usage events (for example “level finished”, “screen opened”, which settings are used) and error reports, together with a random ID created on your device and the App's version number. When you are signed in, these records are linked to your account. They are never used for advertising.

Advertising

If you don't have Gustaf's Journey Pro, the App shows short ads between some levels and offers optional videos for coins. The ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads and measure them, Google may collect information such as your device's IP address, ad interactions, a device identifier for this app, and diagnostics. The App does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and it requests general-audience ads only.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before any personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can read how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Gustaf's Journey Pro removes the ads between levels.

Purchases

Gustaf's Journey Pro is an optional subscription, and coins can be bought in the App. Payments are processed entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active and to let you restore it, using an anonymous app user ID (or your account ID, if you are signed in).

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your device by the App itself. No notification token or other information is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in Settings at any time.

One sign-in for our other apps

Gustaf's Journey shares its sign-in with a few other apps from the same developer, so one account works in all of them. Your account identity is shared across these apps, but your Gustaf's Journey progress is used only to run Gustaf's Journey for you.

Data storage and security

Saved progress, usage events and error reports are stored with Supabase, which provides secure, industry-standard infrastructure. Access controls ensure that only your account can read and write your saved progress.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete your saved progress at any time from within the App (Settings → Delete my saved progress). This removes your Gustaf's Journey progress and the usage and error records linked to your account from our systems, and signs you out. Your sign-in account itself keeps working in our other apps. Usage records that are not linked to an account are kept only as long as they are useful for improving the game.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or California), you may ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email us and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children's privacy

The App is made for adults and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
