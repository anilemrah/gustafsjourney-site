Common questions

The letters are too small.

Open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and choose Large or Larger under Text size.

I closed a level half-way. Is my progress lost?

No. The words you found and the hint letters you bought are kept, and the level carries on where you left it.

How do I keep my progress on a new iPhone or iPad?

In Settings, sign in with Apple or Google under “Save your progress”. Sign in with the same account on your new device and your progress comes along.

I bought Gustaf's Journey Pro but I still see ads.

Open Settings and tap “Restore purchases”, using the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open the iPhone's Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

In the game's Settings, under Reminders.
