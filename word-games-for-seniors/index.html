Word games for seniors: big letters, no timer, a good story

A daily word puzzle is a lovely habit, with a cup of coffee or before bed. But many word games are small, fast and noisy. Here's what makes one comfortable, and how we built Gustaf's Journey for relaxed play.

Free · iPhone & iPad

What to look for in a word game

  • Big, clear letters, and a way to make the text bigger still.
  • No timer. A puzzle should be a pleasure, not a race.
  • Simple controls: tapping or swiping, whichever is easier for your fingers.
  • Fair ads. A few short ads are fair in a free game, but they shouldn't pop up after every word.
  • No pressure to sign up or to pay.
  • Something to look forward to: a story, a daily puzzle, a collection to fill.

How Gustaf's Journey does it

  • Big letter tiles, plus three text sizes (Normal, Large and Larger) for the story, the memory cards and the Swedish words.
  • No clock anywhere in the levels. Play for five minutes or an hour.
  • Swipe or tap. Slide across the letters, or tap them one by one and press ✓.
  • No ads in the first 20 levels. After that, a short ad appears now and then between levels, never in the middle of one. Gustaf's Journey Pro, an optional subscription, removes them.
  • No account needed. Sign in with Apple or Google only if you want your progress backed up.
  • Works with VoiceOver and Reduce Motion, and follows your iPhone's own text size up to a comfortable limit.
  • Even bigger on iPad. The same game, with more room for everything.
  • A real story. Gustaf's life, from a red cottage in Sweden to a farm in Minnesota, with a new memory after every level.

Make the text bigger on your iPhone or iPad

These settings are built into every iPhone and iPad, and they make text bigger in most apps, Gustaf's Journey included.

  1. Bigger text everywhereOpen Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size and drag the slider to the right.
  2. Even biggerOpen Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Larger Text, turn on Larger Accessibility Sizes and drag the slider.
  3. Thicker lettersTurn on Settings → Display & Brightness → Bold Text.
  4. Inside the gameTap the gear on the home screen, then choose a text size: Normal, Large or Larger.

Meet GustafGustaf is 80 years old when the story begins, and he's in no hurry either. Every level ends with one of his memories, so there's always a reason to play one more.

A puzzle with a story

Gustaf's Journey follows Gustaf, a Swedish emigrant, from a farm by Lake Siljan to a new life in Mora, Minnesota. Along the way you'll collect Swedish words, old recipes and keepsakes, and Kantis, a cheerful chanterelle, keeps you company on every level. There's a fresh daily puzzle too, and a streak that forgives a missed day.
