Learn them as you play

In the game, each word arrives with a memory from Gustaf's life, so it comes with a story attached: fäbod with the summer farm in the forest, kyrkbåt with the church boats on Lake Siljan, Amerikabrev with a letter read aloud in a Småland kitchen. Every word you find is kept in Gustaf's trunk, and the daily puzzle adds a Swedish word of the day.
